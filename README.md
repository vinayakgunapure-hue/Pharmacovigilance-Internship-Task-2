# Pharmacovigilance Internship – Task 2

## ADR Reporting & Documentation

### Objective

To complete a structured Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) reporting assessment based on a simulated pharmacovigilance case.

### Case Summary

- Patient: Ramesh Patel
- Age: 32 years
- Sex: Male
- Weight: 65 kg
- Diagnosis: Acute bacterial sinusitis
- Suspected medicine: Amoxicillin
- Dose: 500 mg orally three times daily
- Treatment start date: 01 March 2026
- Treatment stop date: 03 March 2026
- Adverse reaction: Generalized itching and skin rashes over the chest
- Reaction onset: 03 March 2026
- Recovery: 06 March 2026
- Dechallenge: Positive
- Rechallenge: Not applicable / not reported
- Outcome: Recovered
- Seriousness: Non-serious
- ADR type: Type B reaction

### ADR Assessment

The reported skin rash and generalized itching occurred after initiation of amoxicillin. The reaction improved after discontinuation of the suspected medicine, indicating a positive dechallenge.
